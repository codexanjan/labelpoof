# Three-minute demonstration

1. **0:00–0:30 — Dashboard.** Open `/dashboard`. Explain product counts, observed declarations, unresolved checks and potential issues. Show the three labelled synthetic product images.
2. **0:30–1:00 — Evidence.** Open Honestly Good Oats. Select net quantity/price and show the image highlight, original text and official source reference.
3. **1:00–1:30 — Uncertainty.** Select date marking, then batch number. Show unreadable versus not photographed. Neither is called a legal violation.
4. **1:30–2:00 — Review and recapture.** Open the correction form. Show the required rationale and absence guard. Add a photograph through the guided rescan flow; a new version preserves the prior assessment.
5. **2:00–2:30 — Full workspace.** Show the review queue, evidence gallery, analytics and rule library. Draft notes never execute as laws.
6. **2:30–3:00 — Export.** Export report JSON/CSV or save a PDF. Show full workspace backup with images, then explain that images remain local and current findings are preliminary observations.

For a live OCR demo, use your own clear product-label photo and confirm permission to show it. The included synthetic labels are demonstration assets, not evidence about real brands.
